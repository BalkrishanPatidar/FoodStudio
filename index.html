About

A table on Patrakarpuram Road

Ballu Da Dhaba is on the first floor at A-1/22, Viram Khand, Gomti Nagar. The room is listed as indoor seating, less noisy, and open for lunch and dinner.

The cooking people come back for is South Indian, especially the Karnataka dosai: Manina Maga, benne, Byadagi chilli and Davangere. Beside that are tiffin, rice plates, a vegetarian Chinese list, filter coffee and three sweets.

Guests writing about the place talk about a calm room, a welcome glass of buttermilk, and being told to order the filter coffee. Takeaway and home delivery are listed, along with family seating and a board-game corner.

  • Vegetarian only, with Jain food listed
  • Elevator up to the first floor
  • Valet parking listed
  • Kid friendly, and open for brunch as well as dinner
